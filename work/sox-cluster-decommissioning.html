05 · Compliance & Governance

SOX-Compliant Cluster Decommissioning

A reusable teardown framework where the audit trail is a byproduct of execution, not archaeology.

  • SOXcompliant governance
  • EKSplus EC2, RDS, and EBS assets
  • Reusableframework, not a one-off

Problem

Decommissioning production infrastructure in a SOX-regulated environment is mostly not a technical problem. The teardown itself is straightforward. Proving to auditors that the right approvals happened, the right stakeholders were notified, and nothing was destroyed that shouldn't have been is where these projects stall for months — which is why dead clusters keep running and keep costing money.

Approach

Designed an end-to-end process covering governance, documentation, stakeholder coordination, and technical teardown, structured so the audit evidence is produced as the work happens rather than reconstructed afterward. Then generalized it into reusable frameworks for both EKS clusters and standalone cloud assets — EC2, RDS, EBS — so the next decommission is a checklist execution rather than a project with a kickoff meeting.

Impact

Repeatable, audit-ready decommissioning. Spend recovered from retired infrastructure without the compliance exposure that normally keeps unused clusters alive indefinitely.
